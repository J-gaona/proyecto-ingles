Grammar & Linguistic Integration Reference

This prototype demonstrates how grammatical rules (countable vs. uncountable nouns) enhance UX logic and inventory clarity:

✓ 3x "How many...?"
  • Welcome: "How many items do you want to add to your pantry today?"
  • Quantity: "How many [Apples/Bananas/Eggs...] would you like to buy?"
  • Checkout: "How many eco-friendly bags do you need for this order?"
✓ 3x "How much...?"
  • Quantity: "How much [Milk/Rice/Cheese...] do you need for your recipe?"
  • Availability: "...how much rice is left in the warehouse..."
  • Order Summary: "How much will your fresh grocery delivery cost?"
✓ 2x Affirmative "some"
  • Hero Notice: "We still have some fresh milk and cheese ready for dispatch..."
  • Cart Status: "You have added some delicious items to your shopping cart."
✓ 2x "any" (Negative / Interrogative)
  • Negative: "Sorry, we don't have any bread left in our store today!"
  • Interrogative: "Do you need any additional groceries before checking out?"
✓ 2x "a lot of", "much" or "many"
  • Snapshot Notice: "We currently have a lot of cookies and fresh eggs on sale..."
  • Stock Analysis: "There are not many bananas remaining, and there is not much orange juice left..."
✓ 2x "a little" or "a few"
  • Availability Alert: "There are only a few bananas remaining in our crates..."
  • Availability Alert & Chat: "...we have a little orange juice left in the fridge."
